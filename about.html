About C'Tric Acids Entertainment

Company History

C'Tric Acids Entertainment was founded in 2014 by a group of close friends who shared a passion for music, entertainment, and bringing people together through memorable experiences. What started as a small initiative to support local events and gatherings quickly evolved into a professional event management and infrastructure company. The founders recognised the growing demand for reliable technical support, quality equipment rentals, and professionally organised events within Pretoria and surrounding areas. As the company expanded, it invested in modern equipment, technical expertise, and skilled personnel to improve the quality of its services. C'Tric Acids Entertainment began providing sound systems, lighting equipment, stage solutions, event coordination, and full event management services for a variety of clients, ranging from community organisations and local promoters to corporate businesses and government institutions. This growth enabled the company to establish a strong reputation for reliability, professionalism, and exceptional service delivery. Today, C'Tric Acids Entertainment continues to build on its founding vision of creating memorable experiences through entertainment and innovation. The company remains committed to supporting local talent, connecting communities, and delivering world-class event solutions that meet the evolving needs of the South African entertainment and events industry. Through its dedication to quality and customer satisfaction, C'Tric Acids Entertainment aims to become one of Gauteng's leading event and lifestyle management companies.

Mission

To create memorable lifestyle and entertainment experiences through professional event infrastructure, technical excellence, and innovative event solutions that bring people together.

Vision

To become Pretoria's leading event and lifestyle management company, recognised for delivering world-class outdoor experiences that inspire communities and celebrate local culture.

Core Values

Professionalism We conduct ourselves with integrity, accountability, and respect, ensuring that every project is delivered according to the highest professional standards. Innovation We continuously explore new ideas, technologies, and creative solutions to enhance the quality and uniqueness of every event experience. Reliability We are committed to delivering dependable services, quality equipment, and consistent support that our clients can trust at every stage of an event. Customer Satisfaction We place our clients at the centre of everything we do, striving to exceed expectations through exceptional service and personalised event solutions. Community Development We believe in empowering local communities by supporting local talent, creating opportunities, and contributing positively to social and economic development.
